SolfaScores instrument sounds
=============================

These recordings play the songs when "Piano", "Strings" or "Choir" is chosen
under Me > Settings > Sound. How they were made: abu-app/scripts/sounds/README.md.


Piano (piano/)
--------------

Piano: Salamander Grand Piano V3 by Alexander Holm, CC BY 3.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/),
https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3.
Trimmed, converted to mono and re-encoded.


Strings (strings/)
------------------

Recordings released under CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication), so no credit is
needed. Trimmed, looped, converted to mono and re-encoded.

Calibration clicks (click-44100.mp3, click-32000.mp3)
-----------------------------------------------------

Made by SolfaScores (a single click at 0.5 s), to measure each phone's MP3
decoder so the loops line up.
