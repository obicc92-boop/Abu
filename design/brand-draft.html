  • An open music book, like the hymnal or song book every chorister carries: lines of notation on the left page, and a pair of notes on the right that says "music" at any size.
  • A gold ribbon marker hangs from the spine, as in a missal or hymn book: the one touch of gold, the brand's celebration colour.
  • Faint sound rings from the motif sit behind. They disappear at small sizes, so the icon stays simple where it has to.
  • An earlier draft used four rising bars for the four voice parts; it was dropped because it looked like a phone's signal-strength icon.

Motif

One repeat, shown twice the size
  • Rings and dots from adire, the Yoruba resist-dyed cloth, and fine lines and small stars from uli, the Igbo line art. It's drawn as lines only, so it stays light.
  • Where the tiles meet, the corner arcs join into sets of sound rings: music spreading out from the voice.
  • One colour, at low strength: white at about 10% on red, gold at about 28% on sand. On cards with text, it sits at the right and fades out before the words (white about 16%, or red about 30% on the dark Perform page).
  • Only on the welcome screen, the card for the next service, and empty screens. Never behind the score, the words or lists, where it would get in the way of reading.
  • A Nigerian illustrator could redraw it by hand later; the rules above would stay.
